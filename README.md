# ⚛️ quantum-ratta-machine

> *"E = mc² where E = exam anxiety, m = marks I needed, c = chai consumed at 3am"*

A beautifully overengineered glassmorphic Physics PUE guide for 1st year B.Tech 
students who'd rather spend 6 hours making notes look pretty than actually studying.

Built with pure HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks were harmed. No syllabus was completed.

## 🤌 What is this?

Engineering Physics (MAS101/MAS201) — Most Important Questions guide for MIET 
B.Tech Semester I students.

Covers all 5 units:
- Unit 1 → Relativistic Mechanics (Einstein ki duniya)
- Unit 2 → Quantum Mechanics (particle ka existential crisis)
- Unit 3 → Optics (light ka drama)
- Unit 4 → Lasers & Optical Fibers (science ka torch)
- Unit 5 → Superconductors & Nanomaterials (chhota packet, bada dhamaka)

Target score: 50-55/70. Expectation: pass ho jao bas 🙏
